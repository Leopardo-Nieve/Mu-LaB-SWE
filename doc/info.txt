Modified the inital conditions to permit some flow and allow us to perform an convergence analysis. 
However, there seems to be a non-physical accumulation in the corners. It is very slight, but noticeable. My guess is there is a bit of momentum which is fed there from the initial flow, but isn't able to dissipate, so it concentrates there until instability is reached.
This seems to follow from these two observations:
1. The lower the amplitude of the initial condition variation, the more timesteps it takes to create NaNs;
2. When there is no variation in the initial condition, no instability is reached, even after 1 x 10^^5 timesteps.
